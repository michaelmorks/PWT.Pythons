PWT Pythons Locality Preservation Project

The In-House Locality Collection

I have always been an advocate for transparency in the Australian reptile keeping/breeding community. With that in mind, I have created a dedicated page. In this space, you will find an up-to-date list of the locality lines kept in this collection, their status, and next pairings.

The Collection

Browse through the locality lines currently kept in the collection. Each tab provides brief information on their status, typical habitat and origins. As always, contact me for more information and any enquiries.

Wenlock River Locality

Morelia spilota variegata

Origins: F2 captive bred

Habitat: tropical savannah, permanent wetlands. Dense rainforest and melaleuca line the river before giving way to sandy banks and red earth plateaus

Palmer River Locality

Morelia spilota variegata

Origins: Captive Bred

Habitat: tropical savannah, sandstone outcrops and a network of spring fed creeks. Open woodlands allow for widespread kangaroo grass flourishing and drying with the seasons.

Archer River Locality

Morelia spilota variegata

Origins: Captive Bred

Habitat: dense melaleuca and rainforest line the banks of this seasonal river, flowing from tropical rainforests through eucalyptus woodlands before reaching salt flats, coastal flood plains and estuarine mangrove forests.

Mareeba locality

Morelia spilota variegata

Origins: Captive Bred

Habitat: wet tropics give way to open eucalypt woodlands, grassy savannah plateaus. Large granite outcrops and rock formations create a mosaic of habitats segmented by agricultural land and expansive crop and cattle farming.

Atherton Locality

Morelia spilota cheynei

Origins: captive bred. Curtain Fig NP

Habitat: complex tropical vine forest with semi-sparse canopy cover, supports dense vine and foliage growth across the fores floor. Isolated by agricultural land and historic deforestation

Brisbane Distribution

Morelia spilota mcdowelli

Origins: Wild. Translocated via freight

Habitat: eucalyptus woodland and open grasslands fragmented b rbanisation and habitat loss. Carpet Pythons of this region hav adapted to thrive in both natural and unnatural habitats.

Proserpine Locality (In Quarantine)

Morelia spilota mcdowelli

Origins: Captive Bred

Habitat: low elevation rainforests gives way to open eucalyptus and acacia forests, as well as grasslands alongside rocky terrain. Ancient volcanic activity result in a mineral rich region heavily dominated by basalt and granite.

Pilliga Locality (In Quarantine)

Morelia spilota metcalfei

Origins: Captive Bred

Habitat: sclerophyll forest and cypress woodlands on sandy soil. Ancient sandstone formations provide caves and crevices. Underground freshwater aquifers create limited waterholes along a network of sandy creeks and rivers that otherwise flow only after heavy rain.
