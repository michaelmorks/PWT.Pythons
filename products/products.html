The Anti-Poaching Guarantee

I do not support poaching, and you should not either. The illegal wildlife trade is one of the largest in organised crime around the world, and Australia is absolutely no exception.

Reptiles and birds are the most common in both importing exotic wildlife; and exporting protected native species. However, statistics suggest that Carpet Pythons are not often poached for the international trade, instead they end up in private collections within the otherwise legal Australian reptile trade.

For those who are found guilty of being involved, the consequences for wildlife crimes are significant. Across Australia, fines for wildlife related crimes have increased by thousands alongside multi-year prison sentences following a significant overhaul of federal laws to combat an increase in wildlife related crimes.

You can combat this too! By asking the right questions when purchasing any new reptile, you can ensure that the breeder you are buying from, is selling captive bred individuals.

Ask to see photos of the parents, ask about origins, previous breeders etc.

The guarantee? Any reptile breeder worth buying from will very happily answer these questions. Giving you peace of mind when purchasing your next reptile.
